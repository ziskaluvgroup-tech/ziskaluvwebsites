Common questions.

Do I have to buy monthly maintenance?

No. Maintenance is optional. Your website does not require a monthly EcoBlu/Ziska Luv maintenance plan just because we designed it.

Will I be able to control my website?

Yes. After the agreed final payment and handover, you receive the level of ownership/admin access included in your project.

Can you redesign an existing business website?

Yes. Redesign work can be quoted based on the existing site, content, and what needs to change.

Can you make a website for my type of business?

Most likely. The design is adapted to the business itself — its services, audience, branding, and customer journey.
