Common questions.

What if I don’t have written content?

Send your service list, business details, and a few notes about what customers should know. We can help organize text you provide. If you need writing from scratch, we will quote that separately before starting; you approve the final wording.

What if I don’t have photos or a logo?

We can discuss suitable licensed stock photos or a design that works without business photography. Paid images and logo design are separate unless included in your written quote. Stock photos will not be presented as pictures of your own work.

How do revisions work?

Starter includes one revision round; Business and Business Plus include two. A round means one combined list of feedback on the private preview, within the agreed scope. Extra rounds, new pages, or new features are quoted before additional work.

How long will my website take?

Starter is estimated at 5–7 business days, Business at 7–10, and Business Plus at 10–15. The clock starts after your deposit and all required content are received. Weekends, holidays, delayed feedback, and extra scope can extend delivery. Custom timelines are agreed in your proposal.

Can I manage the website myself afterward?

We explain the editing options for the selected platform before building. After final payment, you receive the agreed project files and account access, plus handover guidance. A simple coded site may require help for edits; a content-management system, if agreed in your quote, provides an editing dashboard.

Do I need a monthly care plan?

No. Both plans are optional. Standard Care is $49/month for up to two small update requests within 30 minutes total and monthly public-page, link, and form checks. Advanced Care is $99/month with up to 60 minutes of updates, monthly performance and basic SEO checks, and a traffic summary when analytics access is available. Hosting, domains, paid tools, and larger changes are separate.

What happens before I pay?

We discuss your needs and confirm the scope, price, delivery estimate, and any ongoing costs in writing. You receive an order number, then pay the agreed 50% deposit. The remaining 50% is due after approval and before launch and handover.

Are the demo websites real client projects?

No. Northline Home Services, Island Kitchen, and Form Studio are fictional concepts created to demonstrate our design approach. Their text, menus, and project examples are illustrative. They do not represent completed customer work or endorsements.
